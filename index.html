MW4 carries two perspectives: a young soldier living through the invasion, and Price operating beyond the rules he once upheld. His hunt for revenge draws him toward a weapon that could change the balance of power—and into the forces behind the Korean invasion.

The story connection is not just a familiar face returning. It is the consequence of Price’s final choice in MWIII becoming the starting condition of the next war.
